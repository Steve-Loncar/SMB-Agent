1) URL CONSTRAINT (NO HALLUCINATION)
- You may ONLY choose images from the provided input list {visual_images}.
- Every selected URL must appear verbatim in that list.

2) USE THE ENRICHED METADATA

Use this data — do not ignore it:
- Prioritise images where recommended_use is "background" or "product_focal"

- Must be the strongest single image for the concept: best composition, most relevant subject, cleanest potential for OOH adaptation
- Must be background or product_focal type where possible

OOH is a fast-scan medium (3–6 seconds). The hero image must survive the poster test:
- Bold, clear subject with strong focal point
- Natural negative space potential (sky, wall, shadow area) for headline overlay
- High contrast potential — can carry white or black typography
- Photographic quality — not icons, illustrations, or text-heavy images

5) BUSINESS/VERTICAL AWARENESS
Apply vertical-appropriate preferences from guidelines.vertical and tone:
- Hospitality/food: hero food photography, craft shots, warm interiors
